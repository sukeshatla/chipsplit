Feature 1

Poker nights, start to finish

Built for entering a whole table on a phone, while the game is happening.

  • Start a game with the group's regulars; the buy-in per player is required, so nobody plays on a stale amount.
  • + adds a rebuy in one tap; − opens the player's panel: buy-ins (3 × $50 = $150), chips given back, and one-tap fixes.
  • Every tap shows an Undo, so a mistaken + is fixed without padding anyone's numbers.
  • Cash-outs when the table breaks; a live table check makes sure money in equals money out.
  • Finalize to get the fewest payments; tick each one off as Paid. Only the game's host can change it; everyone else follows live.
  • Any member can start a game. Deleted a game by mistake? The host can Restore it, payments and all, from the group's History tab.
  • One table at a time: while a game is in progress, New game points you to it instead of starting a second one. Rummy works the same way.
Feature 2

Expenses, Splitwise-style

Trips, rent and dinners, in a group or just between two friends.

  • Short by default: what, how much, who paid, split equally or one person owes all.
  • More options: several payers, split by shares, exact amounts, or percentages, always to the exact cent.
  • One-on-one expenses with a friend need no group, and can be in USD, INR, or GBP; each currency keeps its own balance, never converted.
  • Anyone in the group can add, edit, or delete an expense, and bring a deleted one back from History.
  • Import a spreadsheet (Excel or CSV).
Feature 3

Balances you can trust

One number per person, adding up games, expenses, and payments, with the rules enforced by the database.

  • Dashboard: overall, you're up / you owe, with You get and You pay at a glance, plus a banner for each poker or rummy game in progress you're playing in.
  • Each friend's balance across every group and one-on-one, biggest amount first whether you're owed or you owe (green owes you, red you owe), settled-up friends last. The Dashboard lists groups the same way.
  • A Poker or Rummy group's Balances tab: Where everyone stands open, Settle up collapsed until you tap it. An Expense group gives them two tabs, Balances and Settle up (with Payments recorded), both open. The fewest payments to clear it all, record a payment in one tap, remind someone by email.
  • Settled-up people are tucked away; groups and games can't be deleted while money is still owed.
Feature 4

Share images & summaries

Clean, color-coded pictures to drop into a group chat, made right in the app. Green is up, red is down.

  • Every picture opens with a banner: the Chip n Split logo, a clear label (Game · In progress in gold, Game · Final results, Game · Settle up, Group · Balances, Group · Settle up), the group, and when: the game's date, place, and buy-in, or as of today.
  • Every card has its own Share and sends one picture of just that card: a game's Players and Settle up (with Paid ✓ marks), a group's Where everyone stands and Settle up. A game in progress shows the pot on the table.
  • The top of a game or group page has one plain Email summary button; one-person reminders open pre-filled in your own mail app too.
  • Only the picture is sent, no extra text; longer lists get tighter rows.
Feature 5

Poker, Rummy & Expense groups

Every group has one job, picked when you make it.

  • New group in two short steps that fit above the phone keyboard: tap Poker, Rummy, or Expense, add a name and currency, then pick friends and create.
  • One clear button at the top of each group: New game, New rummy game, or + Add expense.
  • Rummy groups score pool rummy hand by hand (up to 7 players, out at 101, 151, or 201; a buy-in is required, like poker; rejoin at the top score + 1, which the scorer can change), with everything Poker has: only the person who started it can change it, In progress / Final, Share, History, and a payout that goes into balances by itself with Mark paid per payment. Like poker, a game can't be deleted until it's settled, and the payout follows the result: deleting the game, or an edit that puts it back in play, takes it out of the balances.
  • Add round opens a compact pop-up, two small score boxes to a row, so a full table fits above the keyboard; the round-by-round table runs rounds across, newest first in view. Fix a round with its ✎ and confirm the change.
  • Poker and Rummy groups each have a leaderboard: games, wins, money won or lost, best night. The Poker menu lists the nights you played.
Feature 6

Friends

Everyone you split with, in one list, whether you met them in a group or one-on-one.

  • Add a friend in one short form: With email (name + their Google email, so they're linked when they sign in) or Guest, no email (just a name).
  • Each friend shows what you share: 2 shared groups, one-on-one, or nothing shared yet.
  • A friend's page adds up your one-on-one expenses into one Balance per currency, plus your balance in each shared group, each with Settle.
  • Remove friend works for anyone (friend, invited, or guest), but only once you're settled up. They come back on their own if a balance with them opens again.
Feature 7

Who can do what

Simple roles, checked by the database itself, so they hold even if someone tries to get around the app.

Every memberAdd, edit, delete, and restore expenses. Start games. Record payments. Add people.
A game's hostWhoever started the game runs it: buy-ins, cash-outs, finalize, mark paid, delete or restore it.
Group adminsThe group's creator, plus anyone they make admin. Change settings, make others admin, delete the group.
Delete safelyA group or a finished game can't be deleted until it's settled up. Deleted expenses and games can be restored.

Security

Signing in with Google

Chip n Split never sees or stores a password. Google does the sign-in, on Google's own page.

Tap "Continue with Google"→Google's sign-in page→Google confirms it's you→You're in
No passwords, everYour password and 2-step verification stay with Google. The app only gets Google's confirmation.
Only name, email, photoNo access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or anything else in your Google account.
Private to your groupsThe database itself checks every request: you only see groups you belong to.
Encrypted, and revocableHTTPS everywhere. Remove the app anytime: Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.
No money moves through the app and no bank details are stored; it only records who owes whom. No Gmail? Create a Google account with your existing email (Yahoo, Outlook, …) and sign in with that.
